[04]Heat & your battery

Mining makes phones hot.

Mining keeps your phone's processor busy the whole time it runs. The phone gets warm, the battery drains faster, and steady heat wears a battery out sooner, the same as long gaming sessions do.

The app watches the battery temperature and Android's own heat warnings. It warns at 40°C, pauses at 42°C until the battery is back under 38°C, and stops completely at 45°C or when Android reports critical heat. Off the charger it also pauses below 25% battery. It asks you to accept this notice before the first run.

Those limits lower the risk; they don't remove it. Mine somewhere cool and ventilated, take thick cases off, and never mine under a pillow, in bed, in direct sun or in a hot car. Stop if the phone feels hot to hold, swells, or behaves oddly.

Don't leave a phone mining unattended or overnight. The pause and the stop are software: a frozen app, a faulty temperature reading or an Android quirk could keep them from kicking in. Mine while you're around to check on the phone, and stop mining before you go to sleep or go out.

If you can, mine on a spare phone you don't rely on rather than your everyday one. An older phone is fine as long as its battery is healthy: never mine on a phone whose battery is swollen, failing or runs hot already.

You mine at your own risk. Soqzilla is free, experimental, open-source software provided as is, without warranty of any kind. Its makers accept no responsibility for damage to your phone, its battery, your data or anything else that results from using it.
