Privacy

Soqzilla is a free hobby project with no accounts, no cookies, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't sell or share anything. This page covers soqzilla.com and the Soqzilla Android app.

The blockchain is public and permanentEvery find, transfer and wallet address Soqzilla records is written to the Soqucoin blockchain, where anyone can see it and nobody, including us, can delete it. Only mine to or send from an address you're happy to be public.

What reaches our server

  • Your IP address, as with any website. The server keeps it in memory only, never on disk or in its logs, and uses it for two things: limiting how fast one address can call the API (forgotten after about two minutes without requests) and counting how many phones are mining. For that count it keeps a scrambled code made from the IP address, not the address itself, and forgets it after three minutes. The code changes every time the server restarts, so it can't be traced back.
  • Wallet addresses and transactions: the addresses you look up or mine to, the finds you submit and the transactions you send through us. All of these are public on the blockchain anyway. Our server log notes each find it relays and the address it paid.
  • The fee grant list: wallet addresses that received the one-off 0.05 SOQ fee grant, so nobody gets it twice.

We use this only to run Soqzilla and keep it safe from abuse (our legitimate interest in running the service).

Who else is involved

  • Cloudflare carries all traffic to soqzilla.com and serves the app download, so it sees your IP address and the pages you request. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Railway hosts the server and may keep short-lived records of requests, as hosts do. See Railway's privacy policy.
  • Soqucoin's public servers are queried by our server, not by your phone or browser, so they never see your IP address.
  • Telegram only if you follow the contact link.

The website loads nothing from other sites: its fonts and scripts come from soqzilla.com.

On your phone

  • The app keeps your wallet key on the phone, locked with your passphrase and Android's secure key storage. It is never sent to us or anyone else, and the app is excluded from Android's cloud backups.
  • The app has no analytics, crash reporting, ads or trackers. It talks only to soqzilla.com (and, if that is unreachable, the same server's backup address).
  • The website stores nothing on your device.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, through Telegram @Hung0v3r. In practice the only lasting record that points to you is your wallet address: we can remove it from the fee grant list, but we can't remove anything from the blockchain. If you're in the EU you can complain to your data protection authority; in Ireland that is the Data Protection Commission.
